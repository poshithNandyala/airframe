Why Airframe

A browser tab, without the browser.

A normal tab disappears behind your work. Picture-in-picture gives you a tiny box with no controls. Airframe is a real YouTube window that behaves like part of your desktop.

Always on top

No title bar, taskbar button or tray icon. Airframe behaves like a screen, not an app.

Pinned by default

Display mode

AltL sends clicks, scroll, drag and hover to the app behind the video. Bring that app to the front first.

True click-through

Any opacity

0–100%. Fade it to a hint over your editor, or bring it back with AltShift↑.

Alt + Shift + arrows

Screen-share shield

Excluded from Teams, Zoom, Meet, OBS and most screenshots. On by default.

Windows display affinity

Shortcuts that don't stick

Alt chords are held back until Airframe knows what you meant. No menu bars, no stuck keys.

FAQ

Good to know.

Everything else is in the README.

Is Airframe free?

Yes. Airframe is free and open source under the MIT license. The source is on GitHub.

Which screens are supported?

YouTube is ready today. Prime Video, Netflix and Twitch are marked coming soon in the launcher.

How does click-through work?

Press AltL. Airframe becomes display-only and your clicks, wheel, drag and typing go to the top window behind it. Bring that app to the front first. Press the shortcut again to take the window back.

Will it hide from screen share?

The shield uses Windows display affinity and is on by default. Teams, Zoom, Meet, OBS and most screenshots exclude the window. A few capture tools may behave differently.

Where is my YouTube sign-in stored?

In %APPDATA%\Airframe on your PC. There is no Airframe account, server or telemetry. Sign in once and the session is remembered.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC." Click More info → Run anyway. The source is public if you want to inspect it first.

Does it run on macOS or Linux?

No. Airframe is built for Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit only.

Can I use YouTube Premium?

Yes — sign in as usual. Normal YouTube videos play. Rented movies and DRM-only content may not, because Electron does not ship Google Widevine.
