Best floating YouTube player for Windows

Dedicated player, browser PiP, or a window utility? The right answer depends on whether you want a quick clip, a pinned browser, or a YouTube window that lives on your desktop.

By Poshith Nandyala Updated November 25, 2026 4 min read
Short answer

For a dedicated, privacy-focused YouTube window on Windows, Airframe is the best fit: free, open source, always on top, with opacity, click-through and a screen-share shield. Browser PiP wins when you want zero install; PowerToys wins when you need to pin any app.

There is no shortage of ways to float a YouTube video on Windows. The right choice depends on whether you want a quick clip, a pinned browser, a general-purpose window utility, or a dedicated player that behaves like part of your desktop.

Disclosure: we build Airframe, so treat this as a biased comparison with the bias stated up front. The table uses documented features and obvious trade-offs; check each project's current page before you install or buy.

How we compared them

  • Always on top: does the video stay above normal windows?
  • Full YouTube controls: can you search, browse playlists and stay signed in?
  • Opacity: can you fade the video without closing it?
  • Click-through: can your mouse work in the app behind the video?
  • Screen-share shield: can the window be excluded from capture?
  • Global shortcuts: can you control it while another app has focus?
  • Price and license: free, open source, paid, subscription?
  • Platform: Windows only or cross-platform?

Airframe: best for work and privacy

Airframe is the only tool in this list built specifically around YouTube as a desktop window. It opens the real YouTube site in a borderless window, removes the title bar and taskbar button, and adds the controls that make a video livable on a work desktop:

  • 0–100% opacity, with AltShift↑ and ↓.
  • True click-through with AltL, including WinUI 3 and WebView2 targets.
  • Screen-share shield on by default, toggled with AltS.
  • 14 global shortcuts, touchpad back/forward and mouse side buttons.
  • No account, no server, no telemetry. The YouTube session stays in %APPDATA%\Airframe.
  • Free and MIT licensed.

Limitations: Windows 10/11 x64 only. YouTube is the only finished screen; Prime Video, Netflix and Twitch are coming soon. The installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn. Rented or DRM-only YouTube movies may not play.

Browser picture-in-picture: best if you want zero install

Chrome, Edge and Firefox all offer PiP. It is free, fast and cross-platform. The trade-offs are the small default size, limited controls, no opacity, no click-through, no shield, and one video per browser. For a five-minute clip, none of that matters. For an all-day window, it does.

PowerToys Always on Top: best for pinning any app

Microsoft PowerToys is free, open source and genuinely useful. Enable Always on Top and press WinCtrlT to pin the active window. It works on any app, not just video. What it does not do is remove browser chrome, fade the window, pass clicks through, or shield it from capture. It is a pin, not a player.

WindowTop: best for advanced window effects

WindowTop is a general-purpose window utility with always-on-top, opacity, click-through and picture-in-picture features. It is a good fit if you want those effects across many apps. It is not YouTube-specific, and some features sit behind a Pro tier. Check the current pricing and feature list.

Other options worth a look

FloatingYT is a lightweight Windows app for a floating online video window. Glaze is a free Windows YouTube desktop client with PiP and always-on-top options. Both are worth evaluating if you want a YouTube-only window and do not need Airframe's shield or click-through. Feature sets in small projects change quickly, so verify the current build before relying on a specific control.

Which should you pick?

  • Just a quick clip: browser PiP.
  • Pin any app above the rest: PowerToys Always on Top.
  • Advanced window effects across apps: WindowTop.
  • YouTube all day, with opacity, click-through and screen-share privacy: Airframe.
  • Netflix, Prime Video or Twitch today: none of the dedicated options are finished yet; use the site's own player or browser PiP until those screens ship.

The bottom line

The best floating YouTube player is the one that matches your day. If YouTube is background noise while you work, Airframe is built for that exact job. If you only need a video for a minute, the browser already has you covered.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best floating YouTube player for Windows?

For a dedicated YouTube window with privacy controls, Airframe is the best fit. It is free, open source, always on top, and adds opacity, click-through and a screen-share shield. Browser picture-in-picture is better if you do not want to install anything.

Is Airframe really free?

Yes. Airframe is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account, subscription or telemetry.

Does Airframe work with Netflix, Prime Video or Twitch?

Not yet. YouTube is ready today. Prime Video, Netflix and Twitch are shown as coming soon in the launcher.

Can I use these players on macOS or Linux?

Browser picture-in-picture works across platforms. Airframe is Windows 10/11 only. PowerToys and WindowTop are Windows tools. Check each project for current platform support.

Are floating player apps safe to install?

Download from the official project page or GitHub release. Airframe is open source, so you can inspect the code. Its installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn. Avoid repacks and third-party download sites.
